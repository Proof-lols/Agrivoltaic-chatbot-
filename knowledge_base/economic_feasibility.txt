Agrivoltaics Economic Feasibility Framework

Capital Stack:
- Direct ownership: farmer retains RECs and tax credits (ITC 30% baseline under IRA).
- Third-party PPA: lower upfront capital, reduced export revenue share.
- Operating leases for land under panels: $800–$2,500 per acre-year depending on region.

Revenue Streams:
1. On-site load offset (retail rate avoidance)
2. Net metering export at avoided-cost or retail-equivalent tariffs
3. Ancillary crop revenue under elevated structures
4. Carbon or ecosystem service credits (emerging markets)

Sensitivity Variables:
- Module efficiency (20–23% mono-PERC typical)
- System capacity factor (18–28% fixed-tilt; up to 32% tracking)
- Crop yield penalty (0–35% depending on shade fraction and cultivar)
- O&M: $15–$25/kW-year

Planning Actions:
1. Commission solar resource assessment (TMY3 or local irradiance dataset)
2. Run crop shade tolerance trials on 0.5–2 acre pilot blocks
3. Engage utility interconnection pre-application
4. Model dual revenue NPV over 25-year asset life
